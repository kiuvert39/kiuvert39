## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Kliuvert Egbe**, a Software Engineer with a background in Computer Science and approximately three years of experience working across software development, backend engineering, and infrastructure operations.

My journey in technology has been driven by a genuine interest in understanding how software systems work, how they communicate, and how to make them reliable, scalable, and maintainable.

I enjoy working beyond just writing application code. From designing backend services and managing databases to provisioning cloud infrastructure, configuring servers, and automating deployment pipelines, I appreciate the engineering processes that bring software products to life.

Currently, my professional work is particularly focused on **DevOps and Cloud Infrastructure**, alongside my continued development in backend engineering.

### What I Do

- ⚙️ **Backend Engineering:** Building APIs, backend services, and application infrastructure using Node.js, TypeScript, and Python.
- ☁️ **Cloud Engineering:** Working with AWS services, virtual private servers, cloud resources, and infrastructure management.
- 🔄 **DevOps Engineering:** Automating deployment workflows, managing CI/CD pipelines, and working with containerized applications.
- 🗄️ **Database Engineering:** Working with relational databases, database design, and data management.
- 🧩 **Problem Solving:** Breaking down complex technical problems into practical and maintainable solutions.

### Beyond Engineering

I'm particularly interested in how technology can address real-world challenges in developing economies.

Having worked around healthcare technology, I've developed a strong interest in digital health infrastructure and the role of reliable software systems in improving service delivery.

I'm also interested in entrepreneurship, artificial intelligence, applied computing, and building technology products that create practical value.
